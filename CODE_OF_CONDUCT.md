# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in WireLens a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, trolling, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this Code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Scope and attribution

This Code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
